# HR_Analystics_Dashboard--Tableau
![](https://github.com/Saswati-18/HR_Analystics_Dashboard--Tableau/blob/main/Hr%20dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze company attrition using Tableau to help HR make data-driven decisions
  
## 👩‍💻 Tech Stack

- **Data Visualization**: Tableau
  
## Key Metrics:
* Total Employees: 1,470
* Attrition: 237 (16.12%)
* Active Employees: 1,233
* Average Age: 37

## Visuals Developed:
* **Pie Chart:** Department-wise attrition
* **Bar Charts:** Attrition by gender and education
* **Donut Charts:** Attrition by gender and age group
* **Heat Map:** Job satisfaction by job role
* **Bar Chart:** Employee distribution by age group

## Key Insights
* High attrition in Sales (57%) and R&D (39%), mostly males.
* **Suggestion:** Exit interviews, mentorship, retention strategies.
* Graduates have the highest attrition; PhDs the lowest.
* **Suggestion:** Upskilling and career development for graduates.
* Most employees are aged 27–42.
* **Suggestion:** Focus engagement efforts on this core group.
* 289 employees are least satisfied with their jobs.
* **Suggestion:** Pulse surveys and recognition programs.
* Attrition is highest among the 25–34 age group, lowest above 55.
* **Suggestion:** Career growth initiatives for younger employees; mentoring by senior staff.

## Overall Recommendation:
* Adopt data-driven HR policies, promote flexibility, and enhance employee engagement and recognition.
